Why I built it

Competition, internship and scholarship notices are spread across a dozen school sites, and by the time you see one it's often already closed.

What I built

  • Every day it collects notices from public school sources, fills in the full text, and uses AI to classify them and pull out deadlines.
  • The home page has picks, a deadline calendar, bookmarks and alerts, and it installs as a phone app with push notifications.
  • Ask AI: describe your interests and experience and it recommends notices that fit. Search combines BM25 with semantic search, so results show up even when the wording doesn't match exactly.
  • The home page and category art feature a hand-drawn mascot, Lei, and there's a dark mode.

What got in the way

  • With so many school sources, one of them is often slow or returns a 503. I made each source independent, so one failure doesn't stop the others, and gave every source a time limit.
  • AI classification has a daily limit, so notices are processed in batches and each result is saved and checked, never paid for twice.
  • In late October I moved the whole site to Cloudflare. After moving 5,228 records I checked the counts and hashes on both sides.
