Cable winch

A winch that lowers an object to a set height automatically. I led the mechanism, iterating in Fusion 360 and finally designing my own ratchet and gears to replace a store-bought winch that couldn't hold position.

When
2026.10 – 2026.11
Role
Mechanical design (Fusion 360)
Control
ESP32-C3, camera tracking an AprilTag
Status
Parts printed, final wiring left

Goal

A camera watches an AprilTag on the hanging load to work out its height, and an ESP32-C3 drives the motor over Wi-Fi to raise or lower it to the target. It can also be controlled by hand from a phone.

What I did

  • I owned the mechanical design, taking it from the first version to Mk4 in Fusion 360 and reworking the layout, part sizes and cable routing each time.
  • The store-bought winch couldn't stop at a set point, so it was useless. I designed my own ratchet and gears: a ratchet wheel beside the drum and a pawl meant to lock it in place.

The team's control software

The control software plans speed with an S-curve and tracks position with PID, slowing down before the target and locking one way once it arrives, so it doesn't bounce back and forth.
