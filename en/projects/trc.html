TRC XY balance module

Our Taiwan Robot Competition entry: a counterweight module for warehouse robots that makes tall loads less likely to tip when the robot turns or brakes.

Team

Role

When
Since 2026.10
Status
Finalist; build about to start

The problem

Factories stack loads high, so warehouse robots risk tipping over when they turn, brake or get bumped. Most sites just slow them down.

How it works

  • Load cells at the four corners of the platform measure force to work out which way the load is about to tip and how much margin is left.
  • Timing belts and linear rails on two axes move a counterweight: the Y axis carries the whole X mechanism, and the X axis carries the weight, so it can reach any point on the plane.
  • Each axis runs four 775 DC motors in sync on both sides — eight in total — so the slides are loaded evenly and don't jam.
  • The counterweight moves under PID plus S-curve control, with limits on speed and acceleration so the motion itself doesn't rock the robot.

Where it stands

  • Initial simulation in our 8-page technical report: with the module installed, the static tipping threshold rises by about 11–12%.
  • We're confirmed as finalists and are about to start building it.
